# Car Boot Reseller: Playtest Guide (build 0.11.0, "The Business Update")

Thanks for playing! This is an early build. We're mostly interested in **how it feels**: where you got confused, what felt unfair, what felt satisfying, and when you wanted to stop.

## The pitch

You start with £300, two hands and a spare room. Every morning you walk a British car boot sale, and every item is a small gamble.

- **Inspect** is a quick look. It's cheap, but it can be wrong.
- **Check condition** costs £5 and gives you the exact score and any hidden wear.
- **Research** shows what similar items actually sold for, and what you'd clear after fees and postage.
- You get **one** offer per item. The sellers are people, and many of them are regulars who'll remember you.

Items hide details, good and bad: a first pressing, a hallmark under the tarnish, a missing battery door, a signature that turns out to be printed, a gem buried in a box of junk. **Purple "?" clues** mean something's there. Checks, your expertise and your workshop kit tell you what it is, and anything you miss, a buyer will spot.

At home you test, clean, repair, research, price and list. Buyers turn up overnight.

Grow from a box room to a garage, a lock-up, a high-street shop and a warehouse. Buy a van and take on house clearances.

Stay in the red for four nights and you're bankrupt, unless your stock could plausibly cover it.

## What to try

- Play **at least 10 in-game days** (roughly 45–90 minutes). The business side opens up over the first couple of weeks.
- Try it on **your phone** as well as a computer. The phone layout is deliberately different.
- Let yourself make bad buys. We want to know whether losing money feels fair or feels like a trick.

## What we'd love to hear

1. **First 10 minutes:** what confused you? What did you ignore? Were the blue tip boxes helpful or annoying?
2. **Discoveries:** did finding hidden details feel exciting? Did the purple clues make you curious? Did any "you missed this" moments feel unfair?
3. **Expertise:** did you notice yourself getting better at one category? Did the Specialist action (for example "Read the run-out") change how you shopped?
4. **The market:** did sellers feel like people? Did you notice regulars, the rival (Gaz) or the weather?
5. **The business:** was there something you were saving up for? Did the garage, lock-up, shop or van feel worth it when you got there?
6. **Difficulty:** did you go bankrupt? When, and did you see it coming?
7. **Phone vs computer:** anything hard to read, hard to tap, or hidden?
8. **The best moment** you had, and **the most annoying** one.
9. **Would you keep playing?** What would make you want to?

## Reporting a bug

In the game, go to *More > Playtest notes* and press **Copy bug report info**. Paste that into your report. It includes your save code, so we can reproduce exactly what happened. Screenshots help too.

## Things we already know

- Balance is still being tuned, especially the late game (shop and warehouse).
- Individual items have category icons rather than their own artwork.
- A few hidden-detail descriptions are shared across similar items and can read a little samey.
