# Car Boot Reseller — Playtest Guide (build 0.10.0)

Thanks for playing! This is an early build. We're mostly interested in **how it feels** — where you got confused, what felt unfair, what was satisfying, and when you wanted to stop.

## The pitch

You start with £300, a tote bag and a spare room. Every morning you walk a car boot sale. Every item is a small gamble:

- **Inspect** is cheap but unreliable.
- **Check Condition** costs £5 but tells you exactly.
- **Research** shows what similar items actually sold for, and what that's worth to you after fees and postage.
- You get **one** haggle offer per item.

At home you test electronics, dig deeper into anything promising, authenticate anything that could be fake, set your own prices and list. Buyers turn up overnight.

Stay in the red for four days and you're bankrupt.

## What to try

- Play at least **5–10 in-game days** (roughly 30–60 minutes).
- Try **not** reading the tutorial, then read it later from *More > How to Play*. Did you figure things out without it?
- Let yourself make bad buys. We want to know whether losing money feels fair or feels like a trick.

## What we'd love to hear

1. **First 10 minutes:** what confused you? What did you ignore?
2. **Difficulty:** did you go bankrupt? When, and did you see it coming?
3. **Information:** did Research / Condition / your estimate feel useful, or like busywork?
4. **Selling:** did pricing your own items feel meaningful? Did returns feel fair?
5. **The best moment** you had, and **the most annoying** one.
6. **Would you keep playing?** What would make you want to?

## Reporting a bug

In the game, go to *More > Playtest Notes & Feedback* and press **Copy bug report info**. Paste that into your report — it includes your save, so we can reproduce exactly what happened. Screenshots help too.

## Things we already know

- Balance is still being tuned.
- Late-game progression (vans, lock-ups, staff, auction buying) is planned but not in this build.
- There's no music yet; sound effects are placeholders.
- Item artwork is placeholder (text only).
